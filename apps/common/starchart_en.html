Date & time

Sends this device’s (PC/phone) clock and time zone to the mount. After sending, check the mount info at the top left of the screen.

Mount coordinate system

Coordinates are exchanged with the mount in JNow (coordinates of date), the same as OnStep, SkySafari and NS-5000. This chart converts automatically from its J2000 catalog when sending and reading.
