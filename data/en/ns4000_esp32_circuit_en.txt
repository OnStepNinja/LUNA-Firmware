NS-4000: basic ESP32 circuit (OnStepNinja_NS4000_v3, 2026-11-23)

The circuit around the ESP32 (ESP32-WROOM-32E) on the NS-4000 board. Every part connected to the ESP32 is in it.
The NS-5000X firmware runs on the same board with the same pins. The circuit diagram is shown above this text.
The motor drivers and the power supply are not in this diagram.

■ ESP32 pins
| ESP32 | Signal | Use |
|---|---|---|
| GPIO18 | RA_PLS | RA motor step pulse |
| GPIO0 | RA_DIR | RA motor direction (shared with the BOOT switch) |
| GPIO27 | DEC_PLS | Dec motor step pulse |
| GPIO26 | DEC_DIR | Dec motor direction |
| GPIO22 | RA_CD | RA motor current down (H: current down, L: full power) |
| GPIO19 | DEC_CD | Dec motor current down (H: current down, L: full power) |
| GPIO39 (SVN) | SEL | Handbox SEL button |
| GPIO34 | RR (RA-) | Handbox button (RA -) |
| GPIO35 | RL (RA+) | Handbox button (RA +) |
| GPIO32 | DD (DE-) | Handbox button (Dec -) |
| GPIO33 | DU (DE+) | Handbox button (Dec +) |
| GPIO25 | TLED | Status LED |
| GPIO4 | BEEP | Piezo buzzer |
| GPIO21 | SH | Camera shutter (on / off) |
| TXD0 / RXD0 | TX1 / RX1 | Serial (USB, RS232C; flashing and commands) |
| EN | - | RESET switch |

■ How it works
- Power: 3.3V, smoothed by 47µF (C2) and C1
- RESET: EN is pulled up to 3.3V by 30kΩ (R2) with 1µF (C3) to GND. The RESET switch pulls EN to GND
- BOOT: a switch from GPIO0 to GND. Hold it and press RESET to enter the download (flashing) mode
- Handbox buttons (5): pulled up to 3.3V by 10kΩ x5 (R3 to R7). A pressed button reads L
- LED: 3.3V -> 4.7kΩ (R1) -> LED -> GPIO25. It is lit when GPIO25 is L (the firmware drives it that way)
- Buzzer: GPIO4 -> 1kΩ (R8) -> piezo buzzer -> GND
- Current down: H (current down) while tracking or stopped, L (full power) during a GOTO. The Dec signal (GPIO19) is given from NS-4000 v1.2.24. The NS-5000X has always given both

■ Notes
- GPIO0 is a boot strapping pin. If the driver input pulls GPIO0 low at power-up, the ESP32 enters the download mode and does not start
- GPIO34, 35 and 39 are input-only pins without internal pull-ups. The external 10kΩ resistors are needed
- All signals are 3.3V. Do not connect 5V signals directly
