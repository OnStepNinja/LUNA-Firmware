Wireless BLE Handbox ver2 (firmware 2.0.0)

A 5-button wireless (BLE) handbox for the NS-4000 (OnStepNinja). It runs on an ESP32 board.
It is distributed only in LUNA Store and may be improved in the future.

== Operation
- North / South / East / West ... the mount moves only while the button is pressed; it stops when you release it
- Stop ... press the two opposite buttons together (North + South, or East + West)
- SEL, a short press (under 1 s) ... changes the speed (slew -> move -> center -> guide; the NS-4000 signals it with sound and LED)
- Back to LUNA Base ... hold SEL for 5 s (or East + West for 5 s). The LED blinks fast from 3 s and it returns at 5 s
- Sleep ... deep sleep after 7 minutes connected without a button press, or 2 minutes without finding the NS-4000. Press SEL to wake it

== Registration (first time only)
1. Turn the NS-4000 on
2. Within 30 minutes, turn the handbox on. It pairs and is registered by itself
3. Only one handbox can be registered. To change to another one, press "Remove Registration" in the File Manager of the NS-4000 (within 30 minutes after the NS-4000 was turned on), then turn the new handbox on
- LED: a short blink once a second while searching; on while connected

== Pins (ESP32; the same numbers as the ST4 / handbox terminals of the NS-4000 board)
| Button / part | GPIO |
|---|---|
| North (Dec +, DE+) | 33 |
| South (Dec -, DE-) | 32 |
| East (RA +, RA+) | 35 |
| West (RA -, RA-) | 34 |
| SEL (speed, wake-up, back to Base) | 39 |
| LED (lit when LOW) | 25 |
| Battery voltage | 14 |

== Wiring
- A button connects the pin to GND when pressed
- GPIO 34, 35 and 39 are input only and have no internal pull-up. **These three need a 10k pull-up resistor (to 3.3V)** (the NS-4000 board has them). Without them the handbox sees buttons that are not pressed, the mount can move by itself and it can return to LUNA Base
- GPIO 32 and 33 use the internal pull-up
- LED: 3.3V -> resistor (about 1k) -> LED -> GPIO25 (lit when LOW)
- Battery voltage: battery + -> 1M -> GPIO14 -> 1M -> GND, and 0.1uF from GPIO14 to GND. The voltage is shown in the Console of the NS-4000
- Tested with a CR123A battery (about 3V)

== Notes
- This is for prototypes that you build yourself. It was checked on real hardware (ESP32, CR123A): connection, every button, stop, wake-up from deep sleep and the return to LUNA Base
- First test without the motors of the mount connected. The NS-4000 stops the mount by itself when the link is silent for 1 s while it moves
- The circuit diagram and other drawings will be published in LUNA Store later
