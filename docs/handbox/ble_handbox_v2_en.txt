Wireless BLE Handbox ver2 (firmware 2.0.0)

A 5-button wireless (BLE) handbox for the NS-4000 (OnStepNinja). It runs on an ESP32 board.
It is distributed only in LUNA Store and may be improved in the future.

== Operation
- North / South / East / West ... the mount moves only while the button is pressed; it stops when you release it
- Stop ... press the two opposite buttons together (North + South, or East + West)
- SEL, a short press (under 1 s) ... changes the speed (slew -> move -> center -> guide; the NS-4000 signals it with sound and LED)
- Back to LUNA Base ... hold SEL for 5 s (or East + West for 5 s). The LED blinks fast from 3 s and it returns at 5 s
- Sleep ... deep sleep after 7 minutes connected without a button press, or 2 minutes without finding the NS-4000. Press SEL to wake it

== Registration (first time only)
1. Turn the NS-4000 on
2. Within 30 minutes, turn the handbox on. It pairs and is registered by itself
3. Only one handbox can be registered. To change to another one, press "Remove Registration" in the File Manager of the NS-4000 (within 30 minutes after the NS-4000 was turned on), then turn the new handbox on
- LED: a short blink once a second while searching; on while connected

== Pins (ESP32; the same numbers as the ST4 / handbox terminals of the NS-4000 board)
| Button / part | GPIO |
|---|---|
| North (Dec +, DE+) | 33 |
| South (Dec -, DE-) | 32 |
| East (RA +, RA+) | 35 |
| West (RA -, RA-) | 34 |
| SEL (speed, wake-up, back to Base) | 39 |
| LED (lit when LOW) | 25 |
| Battery voltage | 14 |

== Wiring
- A button connects the pin to GND when pressed
- GPIO 34, 35 and 39 are input only and have no internal pull-up. **These three need a 10k pull-up resistor (to 3.3V)** (the NS-4000 board has them). Without them the handbox sees buttons that are not pressed, the mount can move by itself and it can return to LUNA Base
- GPIO 32 and 33 can use the internal pull-up. The circuit diagram below has an external 10k pull-up on all five buttons (R3 to R7)
- LED: 3.3V -> resistor (4.7k in the circuit diagram) -> LED -> GPIO25 (lit when LOW)
- Battery voltage: battery + -> 1M -> GPIO14 -> 1M -> GND, and 0.1uF from GPIO14 to GND (as in the circuit diagram). The voltage is shown in the Console of the NS-4000
- Tested with a CR123A battery (about 3V)

== Circuit (the diagram is shown above this text)
- Module: ESP32-WROOM-32E (U1). Power: one CR123A battery (3 V) with a 1000uF capacitor; 47uF (C2) and C1 at the 3.3V pin
- Buttons: SEL = GPIO39, RR = GPIO34 (West), RL = GPIO35 (East), DD = GPIO32 (South), DU = GPIO33 (North). Each button connects its pin to GND. Each pin has a 10k pull-up to 3.3V (R3 to R7)
- LED: 3.3V -> R1 4.7k -> LED -> GPIO25 (lit when LOW)
- Battery voltage: two 1M resistors in series from the battery (the +3V3 line) to GND. Their middle point goes to GPIO14, with a capacitor (0.1uF) from GPIO14 to GND. GPIO14 sees half of the battery voltage
- RESET: the EN pin has a 30k pull-up (R2), a 1uF capacitor (C3) and the RESET button (SW1)
- BOOT: the BOOT button (SW2) connects GPIO0 to GND
- Serial0 header (3 pins) for writing and logs: 1 = GND, 2 = TX (TXD0), 3 = RX (RXD0). It fits the pins of the "LUNA Prog & Comm Adapter v2" (Data in LUNA Store) pin to pin. The header has no power pin: the battery must be connected while you write

== Build and set up (with LUNA Base)
1. Build the circuit and connect the battery
2. Write LUNA Base to the handbox board once over USB (adapter, BOOT and RESET, and the command: see "LUNA Base - writing and using" in Data)
3. Join the Wi-Fi "LUNA-BASE-xxxx" of the handbox, open http://192.168.4.1 and set your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) in "Wi-Fi settings"
4. In LUNA Store open Firmware, choose "Wireless BLE Handbox", press "Download", then "Start"
5. Register it with the NS-4000 (see "Registration" above)
6. To update or change the firmware, hold SEL for 5 seconds to go back to LUNA Base

== Notes
- This is for prototypes that you build yourself. It was checked on real hardware (ESP32, CR123A): connection, every button, stop, wake-up from deep sleep and the return to LUNA Base
- First test without the motors of the mount connected. The NS-4000 stops the mount by itself when the link is silent for 1 s while it moves
- The circuit diagram is shown above this text (see "Circuit" and "Build and set up" above)
